## Summary

- The App **does not collect, sell, share or send** any personal or usage data, to us or to anyone else.
- There is **no account** and no sign-up.

Feedly, Buy Me a Coffee and the 7 apps above are separate companies with their own privacy policies. Healthy Brain is not affiliated with them.

## How the Accessibility service is used

The App uses Android's **Accessibility service** for one purpose only: **to notice when one of the 7 supported apps comes to the front, so it can show the "Take a Break" screen.**

- **What it sees:** only that one of the 7 supported apps has opened. The service listens only to those 7 apps, and only to the "a new window opened" event.
- **What it does not do:** it does **not** read the text, images or anything else on your screen (it is set with `canRetrieveWindowContent="false"`). It does **not** tap, type or perform gestures for you. It does **not** see what you do in other apps.
- **Nothing leaves your phone:** the App has **no internet permission** and keeps no history of which apps you opened.
- **Your consent:** the App explains all of this and asks you to agree before it takes you to your phone's Accessibility settings. Protection starts only after you turn the service on yourself.
- **How to turn it off:** open your phone's **Settings → Accessibility**, find **Healthy Brain** and switch it off. Menu names vary between phones. Uninstalling the App also turns it off.

"Open Feedly" simply asks your phone to open Feedly. Healthy Brain does not send anything along with it.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect personal information or usage data from the App, including:

- Name, email address, phone number or other contact details
- Payment or financial information
- Account names or passwords
- Location
- Health or other sensitive information
- The apps you use, or anything shown on your screen
- Device identifiers, crash reports or analytics

### Data stored on your phone only

To make the pause work, the App saves in its private storage on your phone:

- whether protection is currently paused, and
- the time when protection turns back on.

This data is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

We keep no data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. The pause setting stays on your phone until the pause ends, you clear the App's data, or you uninstall the App.
